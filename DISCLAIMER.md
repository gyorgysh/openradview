# Personal education with your own images

OpenRadView is a free, open-source viewer for opening and exploring your own medical images. This is a personal research project. Use only images you own or have permission to use.

The project has not been clinically validated. Do not use it for diagnosis, screening, monitoring a condition, treatment, emergency assessment or decisions about care. It does not replace a doctor, radiologist or other qualified health professional. Ask a clinician to interpret your scans and discuss your health questions. Do not delay care because of anything displayed here; contact local emergency services if you think you need urgent help.

Images, orientation, measurements, reconstructions and displayed values can be incomplete or wrong. Rendering settings can hide information. Demo collection labels do not establish a diagnosis for an image or person. Software tests and example screenshots do not establish clinical accuracy or safety.

You may connect an agent you choose after reviewing and allowing a session. Its replies are experimental AI commentary and can contain mistakes. They come from that connected client, not a clinician provided by OpenRadView. Neither a warning nor using an outside agent makes a medical conclusion reliable. Do not use the commentary to decide what care you need.

Review your agent/provider's data settings and terms before sharing. The viewer cannot enforce no-training, retention or deletion policies at a provider. A prompt asking for privacy is a request, not a technical guarantee. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
