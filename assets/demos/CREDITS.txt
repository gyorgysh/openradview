Changes by OpenRadView
Only display, pixel encoding, series grouping and physical geometry tags are
retained. Patient names/IDs, dates, institutions and private tags are omitted.
Pixels, dimensions, source UIDs and physical geometry are unchanged. ZIPs use
generic filenames. Exact provenance/hashes: assets/demos/manifest.json.
Retained tags: scripts/datasets/prepare.py. No pixel redaction or resampling.
Screenshots apply viewer window/level, resampling or projection as indicated.

TCIA usage policy and downstream reuse
https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/data-usage-policies-and-restrictions/
Do not try to identify or contact participants or reconstruct identifying
representations. When redistributing images or screenshots, retain the relevant
dataset citation, license, change notice, TCIA policy link and these reuse
requirements. Public de-identification does not prove complete anonymity.
Collection membership does not establish a diagnosis. Educational/research
examples only, not for clinical decisions. No warranties.
