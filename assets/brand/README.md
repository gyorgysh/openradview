# Brand assets

`logo-light.svg` and `logo-dark.svg` are outlined wordmarks for light and dark
backgrounds. Their animated equivalents play a finite introduction and respect
reduced motion. `symbol.svg` is the editable outlined symbol; `wordmark.png` and
`app-icon.png` are the app's bundled raster assets. The layered macOS icon source
is in `packaging/macos/OpenRadView.icon`.

Use the black/white palette and preserve the logo geometry. See
[guidelines.md](guidelines.md). Product wording follows DISCLAIMER.md and AGENTS.md.
