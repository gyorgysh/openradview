# OpenRadView brand

Use the supplied Open Viewport symbol and outlined Space Grotesk wordmark. Keep
the open corner, proportions, orientation and spacing; do not redraw the wordmark
with a system font or add shadows, gradients or colours.

Palette: ink #0A0A0A, graphite #6B6B6B, silver #B3B3B3, paper #F2F2F2 and white
#FFFFFF. Use embedded Space Grotesk for interface text and IBM Plex Mono for small
technical labels. Their SIL OFL notices stay with redistributed fonts.

Motion should finish, respect reduced motion and leave a usable static logo.
Describe personal image exploration and education. The app has not been clinically
validated; do not imply diagnosis, medical reassurance or professional endorsement.
